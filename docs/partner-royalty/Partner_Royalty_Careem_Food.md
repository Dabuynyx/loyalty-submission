## 1. Executive Summary & Market Problem (Jordan & MENA)

In Jordan and across MENA, food delivery aggregators (**Talabat**, **Careem Food**, **Deliveroo**, **HungerStation**) typically charge restaurants **20%–30% commission** per order, plus payment gateway fees, optional sponsored ad fees, and refund deductions (*verified regional industry benchmarks*).

### Why Independent Restaurants Churn or Underperform
1. **Flat High Commissions Without a Path Down:** Small and mid-sized restaurants in Amman (burger shops, shawarma spots, cafés) pay 25%–30% forever, unlike mega-chains that negotiate lower rates.
2. **Hidden Marketing & Cash-Flow Squeeze:** Paying for sponsored listings on top of 25% commission + waiting 14–30 days for payouts crushes restaurant cash flow.
3. **Zero Incentive for Operational Excellence:** A restaurant with 10-minute prep times and 99% order accuracy pays the exact same commission as a slow kitchen causing Captain wait times and customer refunds.

**Partner Royalty** applies the exact same simple, transparent, budget-capped progression model from **Captain Royalty** to **Careem Food Merchants**—turning restaurants into loyal, high-speed partners who actively co-fund Careem's Captain ecosystem.

## 2. The 3-Range Partner Royalty System (No Confusing Tier Codes)

Instead of opaque back-room negotiations, every Careem Food partner sees a **single unified Progress page** (mirroring Captain Royalty's 3-section design):
- **Starts at 75% Merchant Take-Home** (25% baseline Careem Food commission).
- **Progresses up to 85% Merchant Take-Home** (15% Careem commission — **hard cap**, max **+10% merchant gain**; fully configurable per country/contract).
- **Quality-Gated Progression:** Every **200 completed orders** (configurable) meeting **Prep Time ≤ 15 min**, **Order Accuracy ≥ 98%**, and **Merchant Cancel Rate ≤ 1.5%** earns **`+1%` take-home** (Careem's cut drops by `1%`).

| Merchant Take-Home Range | Careem Commission | Quality Gates (30-Day Rolling) | Partner Rewards Unlocked |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **75% – 78%** | **25% → 22%** | Active Careem Food Partner | Monthly Dish & Peak-Demand Analytics + Off-Peak Captain Meal Listing |
| **78% – 82%** | **22% → 18%** | Prep ≤ 15 min · Accuracy ≥ 98% · Cancel ≤ 1.5% | **JOD 50/mo Free Sponsored Search Credits** + **0% Payment Gateway Fee** on Careem Pay |
| **82% – 85% (Cap)** | **18% → 15%** | Prep ≤ 12 min · Accuracy ≥ 99% · Cancel ≤ 1.0% | **Instant Daily Payouts** + **Priority Captain Dispatch** + **Featured Careem Plus & DineOut Placement** |

**Hard Financial Invariant:**
$$\text{Commission Rebate} + \text{Gateway Waivers} + \text{Ad Credits Cost} \le R_{\text{partner\_cap}} \cdot \text{Merchant GMV}$$

| Metric | Standard / Churning Restaurant (75% Take-Home) | Partner Royalty Active (80% Take-Home) | Partner Royalty Top Range (85% Take-Home) |

| **Net Careem Margin / Restaurant** | **JOD 660** | **JOD 1,295 (+96%)** | **JOD 1,680 (+154%)** |

**Why Careem Wins:** Even as commission drops from `25%` to `15%`, **Net Careem Margin per restaurant more than doubles (JOD 660 → JOD 1,680)** because high-accuracy kitchens drive 4× order volume, cut refund losses by 83%, and stay loyal to Careem instead of signing Talabat exclusivity.

## 6. Careem MCP Integration & Technical Boundaries

- **Verified Careem Consumer MCP Tools (`mcp.careemapis.com/mcp`):**
  - `food_merchant_search`, `food_dish_search`, `food_personalized_search`, `food_get_restaurant_menu`, and `cart_get_checkout_promos` can surface Partner Royalty restaurants and their Off-Peak Captain Meal promos directly to Careem users and Captains.
- **Unverified / Missing in Careem MCP (Requires Partner Royalty Backend):**
  - Just like Captain Royalty, Careem's official Consumer MCP has **zero merchant-portal or commission-settlement tools**. Partner progress tracking, SLA telemetry (`prep_time`, `accuracy`), and dynamic commission settlement require a **Partner Royalty Backend Extension** integrated with Careem Food's Partner Portal and Settlement Ledger.
