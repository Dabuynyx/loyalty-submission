# 02 — Rider Progress System & Careem Plus Enhanced Track

## 1. Why a Rider Progress System?

Driver loyalty alone is not enough — if riders switch to Jeeny or Petra Ride for a JOD 0.50 promo, driver utilization drops. By giving **Careem Riders** their own Battle Pass–style progression, and making **Careem Plus** subscribers progress **twice as fast with better rewards**, Careem locks in both sides of the marketplace.

---

## 2. Standard Rider Battle Pass (Free for All Users)

Every Careem user in Jordan automatically participates in the **30-Day Rider Season**.

### 2.1 How Riders Earn XP
| Action | Base XP | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| Complete a Careem Ride | **100 XP + 15 XP per JOD** | Encourages both frequency and longer rides |
| Pay via Careem Pay / Card | **+25% Bonus XP** | Reduces cash handling friction in Jordan |
| Off-Peak Ride (10:00–15:00) | **+50 Bonus XP** | Smooths rider demand into quiet hours |
| 3-Ride Weekly Streak | **+200 Bonus XP** | Builds weekly habit |
| Careem Food Order (≥ JOD 5) | **+75 XP** | Cross-ecosystem engagement |

### 2.2 Standard Rider Tiers & Rewards
| Tier | Name | Season XP | Free Track Rewards |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **R1** | Explorer | 0 XP | Access to Rider Quests |
| **R2** | Commuter | 500 XP | 1× JOD 1.00 Ride Voucher |
| **R3** | Regular | 1,200 XP | 5% off 2 rides + Priority Support Queue |
| **R4** | VIP | 2,500 XP | 1× Free Cancellation Fee Waiver + JOD 2.00 Food Voucher |
| **R5** | Ambassador | 4,500 XP | 1× Surge Cap Voucher (max 1.2x) + JOD 3.00 Ride Credit |

## 3. Careem Plus Enhanced Progress System (JOD 2.99 / Month)

In Jordan, **Careem Plus** currently costs **JOD 2.99/month** and offers unlimited free delivery on Careem Food orders over JOD 5, 10% credit back on up to 10 economy rides/month, DineOut discounts, and priority support.

Under the new Loyalty System, **Careem Plus acts as the Rider Battle Pass**, supercharging progression and rewards:

### 3.1 Core Careem Plus Progression Advantages
1. **2.0x XP Multiplier on Everything:** Careem Plus members earn double XP on every ride, Careem Food order, and Careem Pay transaction — reaching R5 Ambassador in **half the rides**.
2. **Exclusive Careem Plus Reward Track:** At every tier (R1–R5), Careem Plus users unlock **both** the Free Track reward **and** a Premium Careem Plus reward.
3. **Expanded Cashback Caps:** Base Careem Plus gives 10% credit back on up to 10 economy rides/month. Climbing tiers expands this cap!
4. **Tier Carryover Protection:** Careem Plus members only drop **1 tier** on season reset (standard riders drop 2 tiers).

### 3.2 Careem Plus Exclusive Track Rewards

| Tier | Standard XP Needed | Effective Rides (w/ 2x XP) | Careem Plus Exclusive Reward (On Top of Free Track) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **R1** | 0 XP | 0 | **Instant 2x XP**, Priority Support, 10% cashback on 10 rides |
| **R2** | 500 XP | ~2 rides | **1× Surge Shield Token** (waives surge pricing on 1 ride) |
| **R3** | 1,200 XP | ~4–5 rides | **Cashback Cap Upgraded:** 12% credit back on up to **14 rides/mo** |
| **R4** | 2,500 XP | ~9–10 rides | **Priority Captain Matching** (matched with T4–T6 top-rated drivers first) |
| **R5** | 4,500 XP | ~16 rides | **15% credit back on up to 20 rides/mo** + 2× Surge Shields + JOD 5 DineOut Credit |

## 4. How Driver & Rider Systems Reinforce Each Other

```
Careem Plus Riders (R4–R5)
   │  Want fast, reliable pickups & cashback
   ▼
Matched First with Top-Tier Drivers (T4–T6)
   │  Earn 52%–60% split + high ratings
   ▼
Drivers Stay Loyal to Careem (No Switching to Jeeny/Petra Ride)
   │  Higher supply in Amman = shorter wait times
   ▼
More Riders Subscribe to Careem Plus (JOD 2.99/mo)
```

This creates a **two-sided flywheel**: Careem Plus riders get better service from Elite drivers, and Elite drivers get higher-value, cashless, high-rating Careem Plus riders.
