# 03 — Royalty Captain Loyalty & Progression System

**Design Philosophy:** Inspired by Fortnite Battle Pass progression, engineered for **professional dignity, financial transparency, and road safety**—never gambling or fatigue-inducing crunch.

---

## 1. The 6-Tier Split & Progression Table

Every Captain starts at **Tier 1 (60% Careem / 40% Driver)** and progresses over a **30-day Season** up to the hard cap of **Tier 6 (40% Careem / 60% Driver)**.

| Tier | Title | Careem % | Driver % | Season XP | Quality Gates (30-Day Rolling) | Core Unlocks |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **T1** | Rookie | **60%** | **40%** | 0 XP | Active LTRC Permit | Base dispatch, Daily Missions |
| **T2** | Challenger | **56%** | **44%** | 1,000 XP | Rating ≥ 4.50 · Cancel ≤ 10% | +4% split, Partner car-wash & oil discount |
| **T3** | Contender | **52%** | **48%** | 2,500 XP | Rating ≥ 4.60 · Cancel ≤ 8% | +8% split, **1× Family Ride/Food Code (JOD 3)** |
| **T4** | Pro | **48%** | **52%** | 5,000 XP | Rating ≥ 4.70 · Cancel ≤ 6% | +12% split, Instant Careem Pay cashout, **Fuel Rebate** |
| **T5** | Elite | **44%** | **56%** | 8,500 XP | Rating ≥ 4.80 · Cancel ≤ 5% | +16% split, Priority Airport Queue, **Family Quik Code (JOD 5)** |
| **T6** | Legend | **40%** | **60%** | 13,000 XP | Rating ≥ 4.85 · Cancel ≤ 4% | **Max 60% split**, VIP Support, **1× Tier Shield**, **Family Education/Health Perk** |

## 2. How XP Works (Transparent & Fatigue-Safe)

### 2.1 Ride XP Formula
`Ride XP = (50 Base XP + 10 × Fare_JOD) × Peak_Multiplier × Cashless_Multiplier`

- **Peak / High-Demand Zones:** `1.5x` during Amman rush hours (07:30–09:30, 16:00–19:00) or Airport/Abdali pickups.
- **Careem Pay / Card Trips:** `1.25x` bonus (reduces cash-handling friction in Jordan).
- **Fatigue-Safe Daily Cap:** A Captain can earn XP for up to **10 active driving hours per day**. Beyond 10 hours, rides still pay full fare, but **XP pauses**—removing any incentive to drive exhausted.
- **Safe-Shift Bonus:** Taking a 30-minute break after 4 continuous hours awards **+50 Safe-Shift XP**.

### 2.2 Part-Time Fairness & Recovery Mechanics
- **Weekly Flex Quests (No 7-Day Grind Required):** Instead of punishing missed days, weekly quests reward **15 trips/week** (part-time friendly) and **45 trips/week** (full-time).
- **End-of-Season Soft Reset:** At the end of each 30-day Season, Captains drop **at most 2 tiers** (e.g., T6 → T4, T4 → T2). Nobody ever resets to zero.
- **Tier Shield:** Protects a Captain's current tier during illness, family emergencies, or vehicle maintenance (up to 7 days of inactivity without decay).
- **Captain Legacy (Permanent Lifetime Milestones):** Lifetime trips (1,000 / 3,000 / 7,500 trips) unlock permanent **Floor Tiers** (e.g., a 5,000-trip Veteran never drops below Tier 3, even after taking a month off).

## 3. Beyond the 60% Cap: Keeping Tier 6 Legends Hooked

Once a Captain reaches **Tier 6 (60% Driver Split)**, they cannot earn a higher percentage. Royalty keeps T6 Captains motivated through **Prestige & Household Value**:
1. **Overflow XP → Family Vault:** Every 1,000 XP earned *after* hitting T6 converts into a **JOD 2.00 Family Credit** (subject to the global Reward Budget Cap) usable by their spouse/children on Careem Quik or Rides.
2. **Airport Rematch Priority:** Dropping off a passenger at Queen Alia International Airport places T5–T6 Captains at the front of the return pickup queue.
3. **Careem Plus Household Gift:** T6 Captains receive **1 free Careem Plus subscription (worth JOD 2.99/mo)** for a family member.
4. **Annual Captain Council & Recognition:** Top Legend Captains in Amman/Irbid/Zarqa receive physical recognition badges, priority LTRC renewal support, and direct product feedback sessions.
